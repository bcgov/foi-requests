What is a legal proceeding?
A legal proceeding is a formal process used to resolve a dispute or legal matter through a court, tribunal, arbitration, or similar decision-making process.
What are examples of legal proceedings?
  • Court proceedings, such as civil, criminal, or family matters before a court.
  • Tribunal proceedings, such as hearings before an administrative tribunal.
  • Arbitration proceedings, where a dispute is resolved by an arbitrator.
